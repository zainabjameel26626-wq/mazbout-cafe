    A garage, one machine, and a lot of questions.

    Mazbout started in 2016 when two friends, Nour and Karim, bought a second-hand espresso machine and set it up in a garage in Zamalek. They had one rule: if the coffee isn't right, we don't serve it.

    "Mazbout" means "just right" in Egyptian Arabic. It is how people describe their coffee when the sugar, the strength and the heat all land. That is still the goal every time we pull a shot.

    Today we roast our own beans, bake every morning, and know most of our regulars by their order.

    1. 2016First machine, first ten customers.
